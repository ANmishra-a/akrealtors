Our read after four site visits: the tower placement and the internal road are the real story here, and neither is in the brochure. Specification above is the developer's, verbatim. Everything below is ours, dated, and yours to check.

## What four visits told us that the brochure doesn't

The approach from Kharadi Annexe is the thing to test before you book. In late July the last 400 m held water for two days after heavy rain — passable, but plan a monsoon visit rather than take our word for it. Internal road width and the podium entry are better than the two nearest launches we track.

Layout efficiency is where this one earns its price: the 3 BHK carries a genuinely usable utility and a bedroom that takes a queen bed with a wardrobe wall, which the same carpet area next door does not.
