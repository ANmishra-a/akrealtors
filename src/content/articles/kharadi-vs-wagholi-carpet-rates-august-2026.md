Advertised rates in Kharadi and Wagholi have converged in the last three quarters, which is not what either developer's sales team will tell you. Registered values have not converged at all — and that difference is where a buyer's negotiating room lives.

Below: filed rates per carpet square foot for nine towers across both micro-markets, matched against sale deeds registered in the same quarter. Every figure is from a public record and every record is linked.

> If you are an end-user with a Kharadi office, the maths stops being close. If you are an investor, Wagholi still wins on entry price — for now.

The short version: Wagholi's headline discount is real on paper and mostly gone once you price the commute. Kharadi's premium is defensible on rental yield alone if you are buying to let.
